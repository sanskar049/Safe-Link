Important:
The 25 URL-structure features alone cannot reliably distinguish an exact legitimate brand
domain from a phishing URL that contains a brand word. The deployed ai_model.py therefore
keeps the 25-feature ML model as the AI layer and adds a separate exact-host evidence layer.
It includes a small trusted exact-host list and a learned list of hosts that were 100% Legitimate
with at least 10 samples in the balanced training data.

This is not a claim that the system can prove a website is safe. The exact-host evidence is
only one signal and should remain combined with domain, server, webpage/content and reputation
checks in the main application.
